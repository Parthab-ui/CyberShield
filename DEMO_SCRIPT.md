  > *"Thank you. In our project, we implemented an educational simulation of a Security Operations Center.

  > Furthermore, we have an interface called `ThreatDetector`. Our `ThreatDetectionEngine` maintains a `List<ThreatDetector>` and demonstrates **genuine Runtime Polymorphism**.
